Guide

How-To

Quick reference for using the dashboard and understanding its data.

Snapshot

Choose an expiration bucket and focus Greek above. Gauges read RatPack, strike bars read Voltra, and the right panel loads BrentBSVisuals.

Timelapse

Choose a historical date, bucket, and Greek. The chart follows Call 1–5 and Put 1–5 strike levels over time. Press Play to move the active frame.

Automatic refresh

Live snapshot sources refresh once per minute with cache busting. Historical timelapse data reloads only when its controls change.
